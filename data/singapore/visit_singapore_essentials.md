# Visit Singapore - Essential Singapore Travel Information
Source URL: https://www.visitsingapore.com/travel-tips/essential-travel-information/

Visit Singapore describes Singapore as having a tropical climate, with typical temperatures around 25–33°C, and recommends light clothing and carrying an umbrella for occasional rainfall. Singapore Standard Time is GMT+8.

English is widely used, while Mandarin, Malay and Tamil are also spoken. Practical visitor information includes emergency numbers, visitor centres and free Wireless@SGx Wi-Fi access at participating hotspots.

For payments, contactless cards and mobile payment options are widely supported. The official currency is the Singapore dollar.

For getting around, the MRT system is a central option for visitors. Visit Singapore also describes public buses as an affordable way to see the city, with extensive coverage and information available through the MyTransport app. Taxis and private-hire services are alternatives when convenience is more important than cost.

The source also provides practical etiquette and visitor guidance, including returning trays and crockery at hawker centres, and points visitors to official information for current entry and visa requirements.
