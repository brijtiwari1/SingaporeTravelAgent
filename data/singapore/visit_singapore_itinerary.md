# Visit Singapore - 7 Days in Singapore Itinerary
Source URL: https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/visitsingapore/en/travel-tips/travelling-to-singapore/itineraries/7-days-in-singapore

The Visit Singapore seven-day itinerary provides useful building blocks for shorter trips. Its first day combines Marina Bay, Gardens by the Bay and Marina Bay Sands. A Sentosa-focused day includes family-oriented attractions and alternatives such as Universal Studios Singapore or Singapore Oceanarium.

For culture and heritage, the itinerary recommends the Civic District, National Museum of Singapore and National Gallery Singapore. A Mandai day focuses on wildlife attractions such as Bird Paradise and Singapore Zoo.

For food and cultural exploration, the itinerary combines Chinatown, temples and Chinatown Complex Food Centre. Another day combines the Singapore Botanic Gardens and National Orchid Garden. The Katong-Joo Chiat area is presented as a heritage-focused district with colourful shophouses, cafes, boutiques and Peranakan cultural experiences.

These examples are useful as stable itinerary knowledge. The final assistant should adapt them to the user's trip length, interests, family needs and current weather rather than treating the published sequence as a fixed schedule.
