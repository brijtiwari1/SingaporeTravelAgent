# Visit Singapore - Plan Your Trip
Source URL: https://www.visitsingapore.com/mice/en/tools-and-resources/plan-your-trip/

Visit Singapore's planning guidance covers traveller essentials, local transport, currency, weather, languages, electrical plugs and practical visitor information. It describes Singapore as hot and humid, with rainstorms that can be unpredictable, sometimes heavy and brief.

The official currency is the Singapore dollar and cards are widely accepted in many restaurants, shops, hotels and taxis. English is the common language for visitors, alongside Malay, Mandarin and Tamil as official languages.

For transport planning, the source highlights public transport and points visitors toward local route and timing information. It also notes taxis and other transport options for visitors who prefer convenience.

The source is useful for stable planning guidance but not for live weather or live exchange rates. This project deliberately routes those time-sensitive questions to MCP tools instead of relying on stored knowledge-base text.
