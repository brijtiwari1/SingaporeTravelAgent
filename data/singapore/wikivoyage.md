# Wikivoyage Singapore Travel Guide
Source URL: https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Singapore

Singapore is a compact city-state with a mix of modern city districts, heritage areas, food centres, parks and nature reserves. Useful visitor areas include the central business district, the Civic District, Chinatown, Little India, Geylang Serai, Sentosa and the East Coast.

For sightseeing, the guide highlights cultural and cuisine experiences in Chinatown, Little India and Geylang Serai; museums and historic buildings around Bras Basah and the colonial core; wildlife attractions around Mandai; Gardens by the Bay; the Singapore Botanic Gardens; and beaches and resorts around Sentosa and the East Coast.

The MRT and LRT form the backbone of public transport and cover many visitor destinations. Public buses provide extensive coverage, while taxis and private-hire services provide more convenience. Singapore is also pedestrian-friendly, although heat and humidity can make long outdoor walks tiring.

The climate is tropical. Sudden heavy showers and thunderstorms can occur throughout the year, so an umbrella can be useful. The guide also describes Singapore as a strong food destination, with hawker centres and neighbourhood food experiences forming an important part of a visit.

The guide includes a three-day Singapore itinerary concept and a Southern Ridges walking itinerary, making it useful for itinerary planning as well as individual attraction questions.
